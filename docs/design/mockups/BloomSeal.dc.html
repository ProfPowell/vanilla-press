Seal 03 · Bloom only

The bloom seal

Six ways to draw the flower, four wax edges, six colorways, a drawing tuned for each size, and the mark at work. Wax and flower colors in the top two rows follow the Tweaks panel.

Suggested system Solid bloom on the 16-bump scallop, in ink, for the logo. The micro drawing for favicons. The outline impression for one-color print.

A · The flower

A1 Outline

The original: drawn petals, gold eye. Light and delicate.

A2 Solid · pick

Filled petals with a pressed vein. Bolder, holds up smaller.

A3 True orchid

Three sepals, two petals and the trumpet lip of a real vanilla orchid.

A4 Sprig

The bloom off-center with a leaf and a bud. More botanical, less icon.

A5 Relief

Petals pressed into the wax, lit from the top left. Most like real wax.

A6 Hand-pressed

Poured wax, stamp a little off-center and turned. Imperfect on purpose.

B · The wax edge (with A2)

B1 Scallop · pick

16 bumps. Reads as "seal" at any size above 24px.

B2 Fine scallop

24 small bumps. More like a coin or a bottle cap.

B3 Poured

A soft round puddle with one overflow. Calm, handmade.

B4 Drip

Uneven edge, one drip. The most character, the least tidy.

C · Colorways (A2 + B1)

D · Drawn for each size

D1 Large

128px and up. Rim, veins, highlight.

D2 Medium

32–96px. No rim or shine.

D3 Micro

16–24px. Plain disc, five round petals.
