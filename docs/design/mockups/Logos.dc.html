Vanilla Press

Eight directions built from three things the name gives us: the vanilla orchid, the vanilla pod, and the press. One shared bloom ties them together, so any of them can grow into a family.

01

Pressed Orchid

A vanilla bloom flattened in an open book. "Press" in its gentlest sense.

Busy below 32px

02

Bean Nib

The vanilla pod shaped as a fountain-pen nib, blooming at the top. Writing comes first.

Strong silhouette

03

Hand Press

A platen press closing on a bloom. Old-world publishing, done by hand and done locally.

Illustration, not icon

04

The Sort

A piece of metal type carrying a V and a bloom. The block is the unit, like the block AST.

Reads at 32px

05

Pod Pages

A split vanilla pod whose seeds line up like lines of text. Content as the good stuff inside.

Wide; crop for icons

06

Wax Seal

A VP monogram pressed into ink-blue wax, crowned with the bloom. Sealed and sent.

Best favicon

07

Breeze Page

A page lifting at the corner as petals blow off it. A nod to Vanilla Breeze underneath.

Family with VB

08

Brayer Bloom

An ink roller laying down a stripe that ends in a bloom. Publish as one motion.

Good loading motion

Lockups
