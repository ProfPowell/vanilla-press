Buttons

Button variants, sizes, groups, and states.

Default Button

<button>

The primary action button. Filled background with the accent color.

Secondary Button

.secondary

Outlined style for secondary actions that should be visible but less prominent than the primary action.

Ghost Button

.ghost

No border or background. Used for tertiary actions, toolbars, and inline controls.

Semantic Colors

.danger, .success

Buttons that communicate intent through color.

Sizes

.small, (default), .large

Three sizes for different contexts.

Full Width

.full-width

Buttons that span the full width of their container. Common in mobile layouts and form submissions.
